## Cellpose (the AI engine)

The file `app/cell_counter_model.js` contains the Cellpose "cyto3" cell-segmentation network (Carsen Stringer, Marius
Pachitariu and colleagues, HHMI Janelia Research Campus), converted to ONNX. The step that turns the network's output
into cell outlines was re-written in JavaScript from the Cellpose source code.

- The Cellpose authors state that their models are trained on data licensed CC-BY-NC (non-commercial). Cell Counter is
  therefore a free, non-commercial research tool. Do not use the model commercially without the authors' permission.
- Cell Counter is independent and is not endorsed by HHMI, Janelia or the Cellpose authors.
- If you publish results obtained with it, please cite:
  - Stringer, C., Wang, T., Michaelos, M. & Pachitariu, M. Cellpose: a generalist algorithm for cellular segmentation.
    *Nature Methods* 18, 100-106 (2021).
  - Pachitariu, M. & Stringer, C. Cellpose 2.0: how to train your own model. *Nature Methods* 19, 1634-1641 (2022).
  - Stringer, C. & Pachitariu, M. Cellpose3: one-click image restoration for improved cellular segmentation.
    *Nature Methods* 22, 592-599 (2025).
